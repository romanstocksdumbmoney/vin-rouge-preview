Regular hours

  • Tue–Sat — 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM*
  • Sunday brunch — 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM*
  • Sunday dinner — 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM*
  • Monday — Closed

*We do not “close” at those hours — those are last seatings. We close after diners have finished.

Holiday hours

Closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year’s Day. Open New Year’s Eve dinner (5–10pm).

Parking

Plenty of spaces shared with neighbor Hops & Flowers — park in front of either restaurant or in the lot beside Hops & Flowers. On-street parking and the large Duke lot on Hillsborough are available after 5pm.
